## Rules

- Use photos Janath Cargo owns, or images licensed for commercial use.
- Generated or stock images must not be presented as Janath Cargo's own staff, vehicles, warehouse, customers or deliveries. Slot 04 should be a real photo.
- Export as JPG, quality around 80, ideally under 400 KB each.
