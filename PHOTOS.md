# Janath Cargo website — photo slots

The site has two photo slots. Until a photo is added, each shows a photo-style placeholder (container steel, a taped parcel).

**To add a photo:** save a JPG into `site/assets/photos/` with the exact file name below and push. The page picks it up automatically. No code changes.

Add `?slots` to the page address (for example `/?slots`) to see the slot numbers on the page.

| Slot | File name | Where on the page | Shape and minimum size | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | `01-hero.jpg` | Top of the page, behind "TO YOUR FAMILY." | Wide 16:9 · 2400×1350 | Container ship, port or containers at dusk. Keep the top-left calm and darker; the white headline sits there. The quote card covers the bottom-right corner. |
| 02 | `02-story.jpg` | "More than a box" | Portrait 4:5 · 1200×1500 | Hands packing or taping a cardboard box at home. No faces needed. |

The route map in "How it works" is drawn from real coastline data and needs no photo.

## Rules

- Use photos Janath Cargo owns, or images licensed for commercial use.
- Don't present stock or illustrative images as Janath Cargo's own staff, vehicles, warehouse, customers or deliveries.
- Export as JPG, quality around 80, ideally under 400 KB each.
