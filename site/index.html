Questions

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does it cost?

It depends on the size, weight and type of your shipment. Send us the details on WhatsApp and we'll give you a quote.

How long does it take?

Timing depends on the shipping option and the current schedule. We'll give you an estimated time with your quote.

What can I send?

Tell us what you plan to send before you pack. Some items are restricted by customs rules, and we'll tell you what applies to your shipment.

Where do you collect from?

From your home in Israel. Tell us your city and we'll confirm the collection arrangements.

Where do you deliver?

To your family's door in Sri Lanka. Share the delivery town with us when you book.

How do I check on a parcel I've sent?

Message us on WhatsApp with your receipt number and we'll reply with an update.
